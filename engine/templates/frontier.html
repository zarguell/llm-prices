How this works

  • Tiers are editorial — hand-curated from the models listed in engine/frontier.json. Model quality is ultimately a judgment call.
  • Parameter counts come from HuggingFace (safetensors.parameters.total) for open-weight models; closed models may have hand-specified values or show "—".
  • Benchmarks come from OpenRouter's multi-arena ELO data (human preference votes across agents, code, 3D, and other categories). "Best ELO" is the highest across all available categories.
  • Prices come from the latest models.dev snapshot. For open models these are typically a mid-tier provider's rates — actual prices vary by provider.
